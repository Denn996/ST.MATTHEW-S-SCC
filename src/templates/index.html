First Reading

Sirach 35:12-14, 16-18

"The prayer of the humble pierces the clouds."

The Lord is a God of justice, who knows no favorites. Though not unduly partial toward the weak, yet he hears the cry of the oppressed. The Lord is not deaf to the wail of the orphan, nor to the widow when she pours out her complaint. The one whose service is pleasing to God will be accepted; their prayer reaches unto the clouds.

Responsorial Psalm

Psalm 34:2-3, 17-18, 19, 23

R. The Lord hears the cry of the poor.

I will bless the Lord at all times; his praise shall be ever in my mouth.
Let my soul glory in the Lord; the lowly will hear me and be glad.
When the just cry out, the Lord hears them, and from all their distress he rescues them.

The Holy Gospel

Luke 18:9-14

"The tax collector went home justified, but not the Pharisee."

Jesus addressed this parable to those who were convinced of their own righteousness and despised everyone else: "Two people went up to the temple area to pray; one was a Pharisee and the other a tax collector. The Pharisee took up his position and spoke this prayer: 'O God, I thank you that I am not like the rest of humanity—greedy, dishonest, adulterous—or even like this tax collector.'

"But the tax collector stood off at a distance and would not even raise his eyes to heaven but beat his breast and prayed, 'O God, be merciful to me a sinner.' I tell you, the latter went home justified, not the former; for whoever exalts himself will be humbled, and the one who humbles himself will be exalted."

Community & Patron Prayers

Recitations used in opening and concluding our weekly Jumuiya gatherings

Prayer to St. Matthew the Apostle

O Glorious Saint Matthew, through the grace of God you left the desire for worldly riches behind to follow Jesus Christ. Pray for our Small Christian Community that we may faithfully respond to God's divine call, live in unity, and generously share our gifts and talents for the growth of His Church. Teach us integrity, humility, and dedication in our daily studies and Christian service. Amen.

SCC Family Commitment Prayer

Heavenly Father, we thank you for gathering us as brothers and sisters in the St. Matthew Small Christian Community. May your Holy Spirit animate our weekly fellowships and Eucharistic Adorations. Help us to support each other in times of hardship and celebrate each other's triumphs. Keep our hearts united in love and fidelity to the Catholic faith. We make our prayer through Christ our Lord. Amen.
